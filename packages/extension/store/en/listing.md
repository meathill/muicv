## Detailed description
Save job posts from any web page to your Mui Resume job board in one click.

Spotted an opening on a company careers page, a job board, a recruiter's post or an X profile that lists several roles? Open the extension and Mui Resume does the rest:

• Reads only the page you are on, and only when you click — no "read all websites" permission, nothing running in the background.
• Detects job postings automatically: structured JobPosting data and Greenhouse / Lever / Ashby pages are recognised instantly; other pages are analysed by AI, including pages with several jobs.
• Extracts title, company, location and location restrictions — remote-only countries, regions and time-zone windows — so you know whether you can actually apply.
• Two ways to save:
  – Public pool (default): adds the job to the shared muicv.com/jobs board. It's free whenever a job is found, even if it already exists, and you earn token rewards for new jobs.
  – Private: keeps the job in your own account only. Charged by actual AI usage; usually zero for structured or ATS pages.
• Clear pricing before you submit: the maximum hold is shown up front and any unused amount is refunded.
• Longer pages are processed in the background — close the popup and reopen it later to see the result.

Requires a free muicv.com account and an API key (create one in your dashboard).
